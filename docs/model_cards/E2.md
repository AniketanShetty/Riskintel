# RiskIntel Model Card — E2: Risk Tier Engine

## 1. Purpose
The Risk Tier Engine categorizes bureau-scored borrowers (Person A) into one of four predefined risk tiers (P1-P4). It is designed to enforce institutional credit policy and support consistent manual review by loan officers. **This is a policy engine, not a machine learning model.**

## 2. Inputs
- `cibil_score`: A single integer representing the applicant's credit bureau score.

## 3. Outputs
- `risk_tier`: The assigned tier (P1, P2, P3, or P4).
- `tier_description`: A plain-language description of the tier.
- `policy_override_applied`: A boolean flag indicating if a hard policy rejection (P4) was triggered.

## 4. Decision Logic
The engine uses deterministic, hard-coded thresholds:
- **P1:** `cibil_score` ≥ 701
- **P2:** 669 ≤ `cibil_score` ≤ 700
- **P3:** 659 ≤ `cibil_score` ≤ 668
- **P4:** `cibil_score` ≤ 658 (Triggers automatic rejection policy override).

## 5. Limitations
The thresholds (701/669/658) are currently hand-authored by institutional policy and are not empirically derived from the organization's own observed default rates or historical performance data.

## 6. Fairness Risks
The P4 automatic rejection override below a score of 658 carries a proxy discrimination risk. Without a formal disparate-impact audit, this threshold may unintentionally disadvantage specific demographic groups who historically have lower average bureau scores.

## 7. Explainability Level
**100% Explainable.** The engine maps directly from a single integer input to an output via explicit, documented thresholds.

## 8. Reproducibility
**100% Reproducible.** The engine is stateless and deterministic.

## 9. Maintenance and Review Cadence
The thresholds must be formally reviewed and justified via a disparate-impact audit. Once the core eligibility dataset is replaced, these thresholds must be recomputed based on observed real-world default rates.

## 10. Out-of-Scope Use
- Do not claim this engine is an ML model.
- Do not claim this engine predicts the probability of default.
- Do not use for thin-file (Person B) borrowers who lack a valid CIBIL score.

## 11. What a loan officer can say to a borrower
*"Your credit bureau score places you in Risk Tier [Tier], which is a standard category we use to determine loan terms. This is based on fixed policy thresholds."*
