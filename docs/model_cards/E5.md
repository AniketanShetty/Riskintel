# RiskIntel Model Card — E5: Readiness Engine

## 1. Purpose
The Readiness Engine evaluates the general financial and operational readiness of thin-file (Person B) borrowers without relying on credit bureau scores. It ensures that applicants without credit histories are handled explicitly and fairly rather than being silently rejected.

## 2. Inputs
Demographic, financial, housing, infrastructure, and business data inputs including: `annual_income`, `monthly_expenses`, `loan_amount`, `home_ownership`, `type_of_house`, `house_area`, `sanitary_availability`, `water_availability`, `young_dependents`, `old_dependents`, `occupants_count`, `primary_business`, `secondary_business`, and `loan_purpose`.

## 3. Outputs
- `score`: An integer readiness score (0-100).
- `band`: A categorical readiness band (`Ready`, `Moderately Ready`, `Needs Improvement`, `Not Ready`).
- `components`: A breakdown of the 5 component scores and their individual factors.
- `policy_override_applied`: A boolean flag if a hard policy floor was breached.

## 4. Decision Logic
E5 is a hand-coded, deterministic rule engine that computes a weighted sum of 5 components:
- Financial Health (35%)
- Housing Stability (20%)
- Infrastructure Access (15%)
- Household Burden (15%)
- Business Viability (15%)

A hard policy floor override is enforced: if the financial health score falls below 0.5, the engine forces the final output to `Not Ready` and a score of 0, regardless of performance in other categories.

## 5. Limitations
The component weights (35/20/15/15/15) are authored by institutional intuition rather than empirical regression policy. 

## 6. Fairness Risks
The engine relies heavily on self-reported inputs. Furthermore, penalizing lack of infrastructure (water/sanitation) or housing type may correlate heavily with regional or systemic poverty, penalizing borrowers for macro-economic factors outside their control.

## 7. Explainability Level
**100% Explainable.** All weights, components, and penalties are explicitly defined and surfaced in the response payload. 

## 8. Reproducibility
**100% Reproducible.** The system contains no learned ML weights and is strictly deterministic.

## 9. Maintenance and Review Cadence
Requires a quarterly review and sign-off by a senior credit analyst. The output must inject metadata (`last_reviewed_at` and `reviewed_by`) to ensure rules do not grow stale.

## 10. Out-of-Scope Use
- Do not use to assess borrowers who have a valid CIBIL score (they must use the Person A pipeline).
- Do not claim this acts as an autonomous approval mechanism or an ML credit score.
- Do not claim it predicts default probability.

## 11. What a loan officer can say to a borrower
*"Because you do not have a standard credit history in our system, we assessed your readiness based on your income, expenses, and business setup. Your readiness score is [Score], placing you in the [Band] band. This is a readiness assessment, not a formal credit score approval, and it helps us understand your capacity to take on a loan."*
