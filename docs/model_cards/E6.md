# RiskIntel Model Card — E6: Livelihood Engine

## 1. Purpose
The Livelihood Engine deterministically maps a borrower's declared primary business type to a standardized macro-category. This categorization assists the Business Viability component of the Readiness Engine (E5) for thin-file borrowers.

## 2. Inputs
- `primary_business`: A single string representing the applicant's stated primary business or loan purpose.

## 3. Outputs
- `label`: The standardized archetype name (e.g., Trade & Retail, Agriculture).
- `cluster_id`: Integer ID representing the mapped cluster (0-5).
- `is_unclassified`: A boolean flag that is set to `true` if the business type is unknown.

## 4. Decision Logic
E6 executes an O(1) hash-map lookup against a predefined dictionary of over 100 business strings. If the input string matches a known key, it returns the mapped archetype. If the string is not found, the engine executes a silent fallback to Cluster 0 ("General Micro-Enterprise") and flags the payload as `is_unclassified: true`.

## 5. Limitations
The dictionary does not understand semantic similarity or typos. "kirana" matches correctly, but "kirana store" might fall back to unclassified if not explicitly in the dictionary. 

## 6. Fairness Risks
Because the engine enforces a strict signature constraint (accepting *only* the business string), the mapping is mathematically isolated from demographic or protected-class features (age, gender, social class), making it highly fair.

## 7. Explainability Level
**100% Explainable.** The mapping is a direct 1-to-1 dictionary lookup.

## 8. Reproducibility
**100% Reproducible.**

## 9. Maintenance and Review Cadence
Maintain a quarterly review queue to analyze the audit logs for `is_unclassified: true` flags. Unclassified business strings must be documented and added to the dictionary dictionary in subsequent releases.

## 10. Out-of-Scope Use
- Do not use as a standalone approval factor.
- Do not use to infer or guess borrower demographics.
- Do not apply to Person A borrowers.

## 11. What a loan officer can say to a borrower
*"Based on the primary activity you reported, we categorized your business as [Label]. If this is incorrect or was marked as 'Unclassified', we can manually update your business type to better reflect your operations."*
